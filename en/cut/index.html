02FAQ

Haircut FAQ

How much is a men's haircut?
A men's cut is ₩25,000. A junior cut is ₩20,000 and a bangs trim is ₩5,000.
Is shampoo included in a women's cut?
A women's cut is ₩25,000 and shampoo is extra (+₩5,000). A cut for hair extensions is ₩30,000, with shampoo extra (+₩10,000).
Can I combine a cut with other services?
Yes. We offer cut + down perm (₩45,000), cut + Milbon 3-step treatment (₩55,000) and cut + scalp care sets.
