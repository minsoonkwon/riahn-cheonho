Hair extensions, volumizing and wigs
in a private room

RIAHN Hair Cheonho Station runs a separate private room for hair extensions, hair volumizing and wigs. For wigs, we consult on custom wigs, women's wigs and men's wigs.

Hair extensions, volumizing and wigs are Director Dain's specialties, and Eunbyeol and Garam also do hair extensions. For cuts and root color for hair extensions, see the table below and our hair color page.

03FAQ

Wig & hair extension FAQ

What kinds of wigs can I consult on?
We consult on custom wigs, women's wigs and men's wigs. Prices are given after a consultation.
Can I get a cut or color with hair extensions in?
Yes. A cut for hair extensions is ₩30,000 (shampoo +₩10,000) and root color for hair extensions is ₩80,000 (Naver Booking ₩72,000).
Can I be served away from other guests?
Yes. Hair extensions, volumizing and wigs are done in a separate private room.
