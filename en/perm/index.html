From volume straightening
to men's down perms

Our perms and straightening come in a basic line and a care line. A basic perm or roll straight perm is ₩80,000 (Naver Booking ₩72,000), basic volume straightening is ₩120,000 (Naver Booking ₩108,000) and care line volume straightening is ₩160,000 (Naver Booking ₩144,000).

Men's perms range from a side down perm at ₩20,000 to an iron perm at ₩150,000. Ojeong specializes in hippie perms and volume straightening; Garam in root volume, curly hair straightening and build perms (CS curl); Eunbyeol in men's perms and down perms; and Director Dain in heat perms. For care alongside your perm, see our treatments.

02FAQ

Perm FAQ

How much is volume straightening?
Basic volume straightening is ₩120,000 (Naver Booking ₩108,000) and care line volume straightening is ₩160,000 (Naver Booking ₩144,000). Men's volume straightening is ₩120,000 (Naver Booking ₩108,000).
What is the “Naver Booking” price?
The “Naver Booking” price shown under a price applies when you book through Naver Booking. For example, a basic perm is ₩80,000, or ₩72,000 when booked on Naver.
How much is a men's down perm?
A men's side down perm is ₩20,000, side + back down perm ₩30,000, and a full down perm (includes cut) ₩60,000.
