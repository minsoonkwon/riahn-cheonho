Near Cheonho Station Exit 9,
RIAHN Hair Cheonho Station

RIAHN Hair Cheonho Station is a hair salon near Cheonho Station with 3,427 reviews. We create popular styles such as blonde and wave perms, and offer a wide range of services including hair extensions, hair volumizing and wigs.

Our booking is linked with Naver Booking, so you can reserve in real time. Hair extensions, volumizing and wigs are done in a separate private room.

Opened in 2025 with 8 styling chairs, we are on the 2nd floor of 59 Baramdeuri-gil, Songpa-gu, Seoul — 157 m from Cheonho Station Exit 9, in the building next to Himart. If you use Pungnap Market public parking (50 Baramdeuri-gil, Songpa-gu), we cover 30 minutes for a cut and 1 hour for other services.

Why RIAHN Hair Cheonho Station

  • Real-time booking linked with our salon system
  • Designers skilled in dandy cuts, layered cuts, parted perms, volume straightening and more
  • A separate private room for hair extensions, hair volumizing and wigs
  • Free parking support at Pungnap Market public parking (30 min for a cut, 1 hour for other services)
