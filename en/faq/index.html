09 — FAQ

FAQ

Common questions about booking, parking, hours and prices.

Home/FAQ
How do I book?
You can book your preferred date and time in real time on Naver Booking. You can also call us (+82-10-3339-4263) or send an Instagram DM.
Is parking available?
You can use Pungnap Market public parking (50 Baramdeuri-gil, Songpa-gu). Gangdong private parking across the crosswalk from the salon (80-1 Seongnae-dong, Gangdong-gu) is a paid lot.
Do you cover parking fees?
Yes. If you use Pungnap Market public parking, we cover 30 minutes for a cut and 1 hour for other services.
What are your hours?
Monday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday and Sunday, 10:00–20:00.
When are you closed?
We are closed every Tuesday.
How much do services cost?
Men's and women's cuts are ₩25,000, junior cuts ₩20,000, a basic perm ₩80,000 (Naver Booking ₩72,000), women's root color ₩60,000 and the Milbon 6-step treatment ₩70,000, among others. Full prices are on our haircut, perm, color and treatment pages.
What is the “Naver Booking” price?
The “Naver Booking” price shown under a price applies when you book through Naver Booking. For example, women's full color is ₩100,000, or ₩90,000 when booked on Naver.
Can I book a specific designer?
Yes, choose your preferred designer when you book. You can see each designer's specialties on Our Designers page.
Do you consult on wigs or hair extensions?
Yes. Director Dain consults on custom wigs, women's and men's wigs, hair extensions and hair volumizing, which are done in a separate private room.
How do I find the salon?
We are on the 2nd floor of the building next to Himart, 157 m from Cheonho Station Exit 9. The address is 2F, 59 Baramdeuri-gil, Songpa-gu, Seoul.
